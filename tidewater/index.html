Tidewater

A block puzzle where the sea keeps coming in.

  • Drag pieces onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  • Every few moves the tide rises one row and drowns whatever is there.
  • Each line you clear pushes the tide back down.
  • Columns only need filling above the waterline, so high water is your chance to fight back.
  • The run ends when no piece fits, or the tide covers the board.
